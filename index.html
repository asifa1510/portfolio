Selected Research

Physics-Informed Hidden Regime Discovery and Local Symbolic Law Learning for Semi-Batch Reactors

National University of Singapore | IRIS Summer Research Programme, 2026

Advisor: Assoc. Prof. Lakshminarayanan Samavedham

Investigated interpretable temporal modelling of the nonlinear Chylla–Haase polymerization reactor using physics-informed selective state-space models, Gaussian mixture modelling and regime-conditioned symbolic regression.

The framework discovers latent operating regimes and extracts interpretable local equations describing temperature-change dynamics.

396,220 Observations
220 Trajectories
8 Hidden regimes
43.4% Test RMSE reduction
17.6% OOD RMSE reduction
