Scientific Machine Learning · NUS · 2026

Physics-Informed Hidden Regime Discovery and Local Symbolic Law Learning for Nonlinear Semi-Batch Reactors

National University of Singapore
Advisor: Assoc. Prof. Lakshminarayanan Samavedham

Developed a physics-informed learning framework for discovering hidden operating regimes in the nonlinear Chylla–Haase polymerization reactor and deriving interpretable local dynamical equations.

  • Generated and processed 396,220 observations across 220 simulated reactor trajectories.
  • Learned temporal representations using a Mamba selective state-space model incorporating physics-based features.
  • Discovered eight operating regimes through Gaussian Mixture Model clustering without manual regime labels.
  • Applied regime-conditioned symbolic regression to obtain interpretable temperature-change equations.
  • Evaluated generalization on held-out and out-of-distribution trajectories.
220 Reactor trajectories
8 Discovered regimes
43.4% Lower test RMSE
17.6% Lower OOD RMSE

RMSE reductions compare regime-conditioned symbolic laws with the global symbolic regression baseline.

  • Entropy-based uncertainty estimation for anatomical predictions.
