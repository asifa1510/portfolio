Physics-Informed Hidden Regime Discovery and Local Symbolic Law Learning for Semi-Batch Reactors

Asifa S
National University of Singapore, IRIS Summer Research Programme, 2026.

Advisor: Assoc. Prof. Lakshminarayanan Samavedham

A physics-informed temporal learning framework for discovering hidden operating regimes in the nonlinear Chylla–Haase polymerization reactor. The approach combines Mamba-based selective state-space models, Gaussian mixture clustering and regime-conditioned symbolic regression to derive interpretable local temperature dynamics.

396,220 Observations
220 Trajectories
8 Discovered regimes
43.4% Test RMSE reduction
17.6% OOD RMSE reduction
· institution
Nonlinear chemical processes exhibit changing operating behaviour that may not be adequately represented by a single global equation. This work investigates a physics-informed representation learning approach using simulated reactor trajectories and temporal state-space modelling. Learned representations are clustered into eight latent operating regimes without manual regime labels. Regime-conditioned symbolic regression is subsequently used to identify interpretable local temperature-change equations. The framework achieved lower test and out-of-distribution RMSE than the global symbolic modelling baseline.

DiffQuantumQR: Robust Adversarial QR Code Detection via Diffusion, Quantum Attention and Contrastive Learning

S. Asifa, R. Sendhil
IEEE iConSCEPT, 2025.

An adversarially robust QR classification framework combining diffusion-based purification, quantum-inspired feature extraction, contrastive learning and entropy-based detection of unfamiliar inputs. Evaluated against adversarial perturbations including FGSM, PGD and CW attacks.

■ paper ·
QR classification systems may be vulnerable to adversarial modifications of their visual inputs. DiffQuantumQR investigates the use of diffusion-guided purification and quantum-inspired feature extraction to improve classification robustness. Contrastive representation learning and entropy-based detection are incorporated to address adversarial and unfamiliar inputs.
