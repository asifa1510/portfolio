Research completed during the NUS IRIS Summer Research Programme.

Hidden Regime Discovery and Local Symbolic Law Learning for Semi-batch Reactors

The Chylla–Haase reactor moves through several nonlinear operating behaviours. Instead of using one global equation for the complete process, I developed a framework that learns temporal representations, discovers eight hidden operating regimes and derives separate readable equations for temperature-change dynamics inside each regime.
